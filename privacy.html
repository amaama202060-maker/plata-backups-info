Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

PLATA Backups is a private tool used by the operator of the PLATA web platform to store backup archives in the operator's own Google Drive account. It is not offered as a public consumer application.

Information accessed

With the Google account owner's authorization, the integration may access Google Drive files and folders created or used by the backup process. The intended permission is Google Drive's per-file access scope (drive.file). Basic Google account information may be displayed during Google OAuth authorization.

Use of Google Drive data

Access is used only to write, verify, list, read or delete backup-related files as necessary for backup retention and restoration. The integration does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling or sale. Backup archives are intended to be encrypted before upload.

Storage and sharing

Backup files remain in the authorized operator's Google Drive account or designated server storage. Access credentials are intended to be stored privately on the operator's server, not in this public GitHub repository. Google Drive data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls backup retention periods and can remove stored archives from Google Drive. Retention may vary according to operational recovery needs. The operator can revoke the integration's Google account access at any time through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Security

The integration is designed to use encrypted backup storage and restricted account access. As with any backup system, operational security depends on correct configuration and management of access credentials.

Contact

For non-sensitive questions about this private integration, contact the operator through the associated GitHub profile. Never submit secrets or personal customer information in a public issue or comment.
